Privacy Policy — Gravix

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy describes how the Gravix app (net.iappscl.gravix), developed by Luigi Casella / IAppsCL ("we"), handles data when you use it.

What the app does

Gravix is a space arcade game: with a star shield circling a planet you block meteors and avoid flying saucers. Levels 1 to 3 are free; the following levels are unlocked with Premium.

Data handled and where it stays

The app works entirely on your device. It does not require an account, we do not run a server, and we do not receive or store your data.

  • Best score and leaderboard — the scores and the names you type into the leaderboard are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They stay there until you reset the leaderboard from Settings, clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Settings — language, sound and background music are saved on your device.

The leaderboard and settings may be included in Android's automatic backup to your Google account, which remains under your control.

We do not sell, share or transmit the data listed above to third parties.

Third-party services

The app is free and contains ads; levels 4 and up are a Premium feature. The app integrates Google services, which operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — shows optional "rewarded" video ads, which you watch only if you want to: to unlock all levels for the current session with no other ads, or to continue a game once after losing all your lives. It also shows, at most once every two finished games, a full-screen ("interstitial") ad when you close the game-over screen: never during play and never in your first days of use; these ads do not appear while Premium is active. AdMob may collect the advertising ID and technical device data to select and measure ads, according to Google's policies. In the European Union, EEA, UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent through Google's standard form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is shown. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — the library is included in the app, but no in-app purchase is offered: Premium is unlocked only by watching the video for the session. We collect no payment data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect data about them. In Italy 14 is the minimum age to consent to online services on one's own (Art. 2-quinquies of the Italian Privacy Code); in other EU countries it can be up to 16: below that age, consent to personalised ads must be given by a parent or guardian.

Data controller, legal bases and transfers

The data controller is Luigi Casella (IAppsCL), an independent developer, reachable at IAppsCL@gmail.com. No data protection officer (DPO) has been appointed, as one is not required for this kind of processing.

  • Data that stays on your device (content, settings, history): we do not receive or process it; you manage it on your phone.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, personalised ads and the use of the advertising ID are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and the ePrivacy rules), collected through Google's form on first launch. For non-personalised ads, measurement and fraud prevention Google may rely on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), which you can object to in the same form. Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects.
  • Emails you send us: used only to reply to you (Art. 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f) GDPR) and kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

Transfers outside the EU: Google may process advertising data in the United States. Such transfers rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, to which Google LLC is certified, and on standard contractual clauses.

Changing or withdrawing consent: where the law requires it, the app's Settings include "Ad privacy choices", which reopens Google's form. You can change your choices or withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before.

Your rights

Because the leaderboard and settings stay on your device, you can delete them at any time by resetting the leaderboard from Settings, clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling the app. For advertising identifiers handled by Google, you can manage your ad preferences in your Android device settings or at https://adssettings.google.com.

Under Articles 15-22 GDPR you have the right to request access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. To exercise them, write to IAppsCL@gmail.com; for data collected by Google you can also contact Google directly. If you believe the processing breaches the law, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it) or with the supervisory authority of the country where you live.

Changes to this policy
