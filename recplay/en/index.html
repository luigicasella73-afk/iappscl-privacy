Privacy Policy — Rec&Play

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy describes how the app Rec&Play (net.iappscl.recplay), developed by Luigi Casella / IAppsCL ("we"), handles data when you use it.

What the app does

Rec&Play records a series of taps and swipes you perform on the screen and replays them in a customizable loop: a number of rounds or endless repetition, a wait time between gestures and repetition of a single gesture (with swipes that can grow longer each time).

Accessibility service

To work, Rec&Play uses the Android AccessibilityService API only to:

  • perform the recorded gestures — the taps and swipes in the sequence are dispatched to the system as gestures;
  • show the floating control panel and the numbered markers over other apps.

The app does not read screen content (the service is configured without access to window content), does not observe what you type and does not collect or transmit any of this information. The service is active only if you enable it in Android's accessibility settings, and you can disable it at any time.

Data processed and where it stays

The app runs entirely on your device. It requires no account, we run no server and we do not receive or store your data.

  • Recorded sequences — screen coordinates of the taps, durations, wait times and the names you give your sequences are saved in the app's private storage on the device. They stay there until you delete them, clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Settings — your playback preferences are saved on the device.

Sequences and settings may be included in Android's automatic backup to your Google account, which remains under your control.

We do not sell, share or transmit the data listed above to third parties.

Third-party services

The app is free; Settings are a Premium feature. The app uses Google services, which operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — shows an optional "rewarded" video ad to unlock Settings for the current session. AdMob may collect the advertising ID and technical device data to select and measure ads, in accordance with Google's policies. In the European Union, EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's standard form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is shown. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — the app offers no in-app purchases: Premium is unlocked only by watching the video, for the current session. The library is kept only to recognise any Premium obtained in the past through Google Play; we never see or store your card or payment method details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect data about them. In Italy 14 is the minimum age to consent to online services on one's own (Art. 2-quinquies of the Italian Privacy Code); in other EU countries it can be up to 16: below that age, consent to personalised ads must be given by a parent or guardian.

Data controller, legal bases and transfers

The data controller is Luigi Casella (IAppsCL), an independent developer, reachable at IAppsCL@gmail.com. No data protection officer (DPO) has been appointed, as one is not required for this kind of processing.

  • Data that stays on your device (content, settings, history): we do not receive or process it; you manage it on your phone.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, personalised ads and the use of the advertising ID are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and the ePrivacy rules), collected through Google's form on first launch. For non-personalised ads, measurement and fraud prevention Google may rely on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), which you can object to in the same form. Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects.
  • Emails you send us: used only to reply to you (Art. 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f) GDPR) and kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

Transfers outside the EU: Google may process advertising data in the United States. Such transfers rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, to which Google LLC is certified, and on standard contractual clauses.

Changing or withdrawing consent: where the law requires it, the app's Settings include "Ad privacy choices", which reopens Google's form. You can change your choices or withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before.

Your rights

Since sequences and settings stay on your device, you can delete them at any time by removing them in the app, by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling it. For advertising identifiers managed by Google, you can manage your ad preferences from your Android device settings or at https://adssettings.google.com.

Under Articles 15-22 GDPR you have the right to request access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. To exercise them, write to IAppsCL@gmail.com; for data collected by Google you can also contact Google directly. If you believe the processing breaches the law, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it) or with the supervisory authority of the country where you live.
