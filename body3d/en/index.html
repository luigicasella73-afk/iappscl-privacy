Privacy Policy — 3D-Body

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy describes how the 3D-Body app (net.iappscl.body3d), developed by Luigi Casella / IAppsCL ("we"), handles data when you use it.

What the app does

3D-Body is a 3D anatomy atlas: it shows a human body you can rotate, zoom and cut through, with the skeleton, organs, muscles, arteries, veins and nervous system. You can tap a part to see its name, search for it, hide it or isolate it. The skeleton is free; the other layers are unlocked with Premium.

Data processed and where it stays

The app works entirely on your device. It does not require an account, we do not run a server and we do not receive or store your data. The anatomical model is included in the app and is drawn on the phone: you don't need internet to use it.

  • Settings — the chosen language is saved in the app's private storage on the device.

This data stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. It may be included in Android's automatic backup to your Google account, which remains under your control.

We do not sell, share or transmit the data listed above to third parties.

Third-party services

The app is free; the layers beyond the skeleton are a Premium feature. The app integrates a Google service, which operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — shows an optional "rewarded" video ad to unlock all layers for the current session. AdMob may collect the advertising ID and technical device data for ad selection and measurement, according to Google's policies. In the European Union, EEA, UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent through Google's standard form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is shown. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app offers no in-app purchases and does not process payment data.

Source of the anatomical model

The model is derived from BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science, released under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.1 Japan license (CC BY-SA 2.1 JP). The meshes have been simplified and colored for the app. Lungs, parotid glands and tonsils come from the 3D Reference Organ Set of the Human Reference Atlas / HuBMAP (K. Browne, H. Schlehlein), released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license (CC BY 4.0), and have been adapted to the model. Peripheral nerves, spinal cord, thyroid, parathyroid glands and lymph nodes come from Z-Anatomy, released under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license (CC BY-SA 4.0), and have also been adapted to the model. The app is a reference tool and is not a substitute for medical advice.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect data about them. In Italy 14 is the minimum age to consent to online services on one's own (Art. 2-quinquies of the Italian Privacy Code); in other EU countries it can be up to 16: below that age, consent to personalised ads must be given by a parent or guardian.

Data controller, legal bases and transfers

The data controller is Luigi Casella (IAppsCL), an independent developer, reachable at IAppsCL@gmail.com. No data protection officer (DPO) has been appointed, as one is not required for this kind of processing.

  • Data that stays on your device (content, settings, history): we do not receive or process it; you manage it on your phone.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, personalised ads and the use of the advertising ID are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and the ePrivacy rules), collected through Google's form on first launch. For non-personalised ads, measurement and fraud prevention Google may rely on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), which you can object to in the same form. Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects.
  • Emails you send us: used only to reply to you (Art. 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f) GDPR) and kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

Transfers outside the EU: Google may process advertising data in the United States. Such transfers rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, to which Google LLC is certified, and on standard contractual clauses.

Changing or withdrawing consent: where the law requires it, the app's Settings include "Ad privacy choices", which reopens Google's form. You can change your choices or withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before.

Your rights

Since settings stay on your device, you can delete them at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling it. For advertising identifiers managed by Google, you can manage your ad preferences from your Android device settings or at https://adssettings.google.com.

Under Articles 15-22 GDPR you have the right to request access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. To exercise them, write to IAppsCL@gmail.com; for data collected by Google you can also contact Google directly. If you believe the processing breaches the law, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it) or with the supervisory authority of the country where you live.
