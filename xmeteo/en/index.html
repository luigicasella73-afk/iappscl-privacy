Privacy Policy — xMeteo

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This policy explains how the xMeteo app (net.iappscl.xmeteo), developed by Luigi Casella / IAppsCL ("we"), handles data when you use it.

What the app does

xMeteo shows current, hourly and daily weather forecasts for the location you choose (GPS or manual search), with optional home-screen widgets.

Data we process, and where it stays

  • Location (GPS or manual search) — the coordinates of the selected location are sent to the third-party weather services listed below, solely to fetch the forecast for that point. We do not store these coordinates on any server ourselves: they stay on your device, saved locally among your favourite locations if you choose to save them.
  • Saved locations and preferences — stored only on your device (place names, measurement units, theme, etc.).
  • Widgets — refresh the forecast in the background for the chosen location, using the same calls to the weather services listed below; no additional data is collected.

We do not sell, share, or transmit the data listed above to third parties, other than sending coordinates to the weather services needed to show you the forecast.

Third-party weather services

To fetch forecasts, the app sends the selected location (latitude/longitude) to one or more of the following public services, depending on the best geographic coverage for that point:

  • Open-Meteo (including Open-Meteo Marine)
  • MET Norway
  • NOAA (United States)
  • BOM — Bureau of Meteorology (Australia)
  • JMA — Japan Meteorological Agency (Japan)
  • Environment Canada
  • Bright Sky / DWD (Germany)
  • RainViewer (precipitation radar map)

Each of these services operates under its own terms and privacy policy, which we do not control. Only the coordinates of the requested location are sent; we do not send personal or advertising identifiers to these services.

Other third-party services

  • Google AdMob — shows advertising (including an optional rewarded ad to unlock features). AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and technical device data for ad selection and measurement. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent via Google's standard User Messaging Platform form before ads are shown. You can review your choices at any time from the app's settings. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — handles the one-time "Premium" purchase to remove ads/unlock extra features. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your card or payment method details.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights

Saved locations and preferences stay on your device: you can delete them at any time from the app's settings or by uninstalling the app. For advertising identifiers managed by Google, you can manage your ad preferences from your Android device settings or at adssettings.google.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app or legal requirements change. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent version.
