Privacy Policy — TrovAuto

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This privacy policy describes how TrovAuto (net.iappscl.trovauto), developed by Luigi Casella / IAppsCL ("we"), handles data when you use it.

What the app does

TrovAuto automatically saves your car's location when your phone disconnects from the vehicle's Bluetooth, with no need to open the app, and lets you find it again in Google Maps, with a history of saved locations, parking photos and notes.

Data processed and where it stays

All processing happens entirely on your device. We don't run a server and we don't receive or store your data.

  • Bluetooth — the app detects when your phone connects to/disconnects from a Bluetooth device (e.g. the car radio) to figure out when you got in/out of the car. We don't collect a list of your paired Bluetooth devices beyond what's needed to detect the connect/disconnect event.
  • Location (GPS) — captured at the moment of Bluetooth disconnection to record where you parked, and shown in Google Maps when you ask to find your car. Saved locations stay in the app's local history, visible only to you, until you clear it or uninstall the app.
  • Camera — you can take a photo of the parking spot to attach to a saved location; the photo stays in the device/app storage and is not transmitted anywhere.
  • Notes — any text you add to a saved location stays on the device only.
  • Notifications — used to let you know when a new location has been saved.

We don't sell, share, or transmit the data listed above to third parties.

Third-party services

The app integrates three Google services, which operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — shows advertising (including an optional "rewarded" ad to unlock features). AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and technical device data for ad selection and measurement. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, you're asked for consent via Google's standard form (User Messaging Platform) before ads are shown. You can review your choices at any time from the app's settings. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — handles the one-time "Premium" purchase that removes ads/unlocks extra features (e.g. extended history). Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we don't see or store your card or payment method data.
  • Google Maps — when you choose to open a saved location, the app launches the Google Maps app on your device, passing it the coordinates; Google Maps' own privacy policy applies from that point on.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights

Since operating data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the history in the app or uninstalling it. For advertising identifiers managed by Google, you can manage your ad preferences from your Android device settings or at adssettings.google.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app or legal requirements change. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent version.
