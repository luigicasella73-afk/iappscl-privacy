Privacy Policy — TrovAuto

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This privacy policy describes how TrovAuto (net.iappscl.trovauto), developed by Luigi Casella / IAppsCL ("we"), handles data when you use it.

What the app does

TrovAuto automatically saves your car's location when your phone disconnects from the vehicle's Bluetooth, with no need to open the app, and lets you find it again in Google Maps, with a history of saved locations, parking photos and notes.

Data processed and where it stays

All processing happens entirely on your device. We don't run a server and we don't receive or store your data.

  • Bluetooth — the app detects when your phone connects to/disconnects from a Bluetooth device (e.g. the car radio) to figure out when you got in/out of the car. We don't collect a list of your paired Bluetooth devices beyond what's needed to detect the connect/disconnect event.
  • Location (GPS) — captured at the moment of Bluetooth disconnection to record where you parked, and shown in Google Maps when you ask to find your car. Saved locations stay in the app's local history, visible only to you, until you clear it or uninstall the app.
  • Camera — you can take a photo of the parking spot to attach to a saved location; the photo stays in the device/app storage and is not transmitted anywhere.
  • Notes — any text you add to a saved location stays on the device only.
  • Notifications — used to let you know when a new location has been saved.

We don't sell, share, or transmit the data listed above to third parties.

Third-party services

The app integrates three Google services, which operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — shows advertising (including an optional "rewarded" ad to unlock features). AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and technical device data for ad selection and measurement. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, you're asked for consent via Google's standard form (User Messaging Platform) before ads are shown. You can review your choices at any time from the app's settings. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — the app offers no in-app purchases: Premium features (e.g. extended history) are unlocked only by watching the video, for the current session. The library is kept only to recognise any Premium obtained in the past through Google Play; we never see or store your card or payment method details.
  • Google Maps — when you choose to open a saved location, the app launches the Google Maps app on your device, passing it the coordinates; Google Maps' own privacy policy applies from that point on.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect data about them. In Italy 14 is the minimum age to consent to online services on one's own (Art. 2-quinquies of the Italian Privacy Code); in other EU countries it can be up to 16: below that age, consent to personalised ads must be given by a parent or guardian.

Data controller, legal bases and transfers

The data controller is Luigi Casella (IAppsCL), an independent developer, reachable at IAppsCL@gmail.com. No data protection officer (DPO) has been appointed, as one is not required for this kind of processing.

  • Data that stays on your device (content, settings, history): we do not receive or process it; you manage it on your phone.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, personalised ads and the use of the advertising ID are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and the ePrivacy rules), collected through Google's form on first launch. For non-personalised ads, measurement and fraud prevention Google may rely on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), which you can object to in the same form. Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects.
  • Emails you send us: used only to reply to you (Art. 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f) GDPR) and kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

Transfers outside the EU: Google may process advertising data in the United States. Such transfers rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, to which Google LLC is certified, and on standard contractual clauses.

Changing or withdrawing consent: where the law requires it, the app's Settings include "Ad privacy choices", which reopens Google's form. You can change your choices or withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before.

Your rights

Since operating data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the history in the app or uninstalling it. For advertising identifiers managed by Google, you can manage your ad preferences from your Android device settings or at adssettings.google.com.

Under Articles 15-22 GDPR you have the right to request access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. To exercise them, write to IAppsCL@gmail.com; for data collected by Google you can also contact Google directly. If you believe the processing breaches the law, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it) or with the supervisory authority of the country where you live.
