Privacy Policy — Don't Call!

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This policy explains how the Don't Call! app (net.iappscl.dontcall), developed by Luigi Casella / IAppsCL ("we"), handles data when you use it.

What the app does

Don't Call! automatically blocks incoming calls from foreign numbers that are not saved in your contacts, following rules you configure (by country and line type).

Data we process, and where it stays

All call processing happens entirely on your device. We do not operate a server and we never receive or store your data.

  • Contacts — the app reads the phone numbers saved in your contacts solely to check whether an incoming number is already one of your contacts (in which case the call always goes through). Contacts are never copied, transmitted, or stored outside the device.
  • Incoming call number — used by the screening service to apply your blocking rules (country code, presence in contacts). Blocked calls are kept in a local history, visible only to you inside the app, until you clear it or uninstall the app.
  • Manually blocked numbers — stored only on the device, in the list you manage from the app's settings.
  • Notifications — the app may show local notifications (e.g., a call was blocked); no external messaging service is involved.

We do not sell, share, or transmit the data listed above to third parties.

Third-party services

The app integrates two Google services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — shows advertising (including an optional rewarded ad to unlock features). AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and technical device data for ad selection and measurement. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent via Google's standard User Messaging Platform form before ads are shown. You can review your choices at any time from the app's settings. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — handles the one-time "Premium" purchase to remove ads/unlock extra features. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your card or payment method details.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights

Since the app's operating data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the in-app history or uninstalling the app. For advertising identifiers managed by Google, you can manage your ad preferences from your Android device settings or at adssettings.google.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app or legal requirements change. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent version.
