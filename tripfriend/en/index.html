Privacy Policy — Trip Friend

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy describes how the Trip Friend app (net.iappscl.tripfriend), developed by Luigi Casella / IAppsCL ("we"), handles data when you use it.

What the app does

Trip Friend compares the possible routes with the weather forecast along the way, shows weather forecasts, remembers where you parked and reminds you of your vehicle deadlines (vehicle tax, insurance, inspection, service) and document deadlines (ID card, passport, driving licence and others). It also suggests round trips with stops and things to see, and offers home screen widgets with the weather and with the Sun and Moon.

It also keeps a refuel log for each vehicle and a travel diary with photos, shows official weather warnings, can record routes with GPS and export them as GPX, export the diary as PDF and save a copy of your data to a file you choose.

Data handled and where it stays

No account is required, we run no server and we do not receive or store your data. Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Vehicles and deadlines — name, plate, description, registration data (power, emission class, fuel, first registration, region), odometer, dates and amounts of vehicle tax, insurance, inspection and service, notes.
  • Documents — type, holder, number, issuing authority, issue and expiry dates, notes.
  • Parking — location and address of the parked vehicle, note, photo, parking time, parking history. Photos are saved in the app's private folder.
  • Routes — stops and history of calculated routes, recent and saved places.
  • Refuels — date, odometer, quantity and amount of each vehicle's refuels.
  • Travel diary — title, texts, places, time, temperature and photos of the moments you add. Photos, taken or picked from the gallery, are copied into the app's private folder.
  • Data backup — only if you ask for it: a file with vehicles, documents, deadlines, refuels, parking, diaries and settings (without photos and without the Open-Meteo key), saved where you choose. From then on the file is under your control.
  • Sharing — only if you tap "Share": the parking spot, a saved route or a diary with its photos are handed to the app you choose. Likewise the diary as PDF and routes as GPX are saved where you choose. We do not send them to any server.
  • Recorded routes — only if you start a recording: position and time of the GPS points detected until you stop it, saved in the app's private storage.
  • Vehicle photos — the photo you choose for each vehicle, copied into the app's private folder.
  • Bluetooth — the names and addresses of the Bluetooth devices you choose to link to a vehicle, to save the parking spot when they disconnect; the name of the device connected at that moment, to offer to link it to a vehicle; the time each vehicle was last connected.
  • Trip suggestions — the suggestion settings and, in a cache, the towns and altitudes already looked up, so the same requests are not repeated.
  • Widgets — the place shown in the widgets (coordinates and name, the same as the Weather tab), its time zone and the last weather downloaded.
  • Settings — language, theme, icons, weather sources and sections, units, any Open-Meteo key you enter yourself.

This data stays until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall it. It may be included in Android's automatic backup to your Google account, which remains under your control. We do not sell or share this data.

Location

The app uses the device location to: start from where you are, show the local weather, save your parking spot, find nearby fuel stations and services, and suggest the vehicle's region. If you turn on automatic saving with Bluetooth, the app may detect the location even when it is closed, only at the moment the vehicle's Bluetooth disconnects, to save the parking spot; this requires the "Allow all the time" permission, which you can revoke at any time.

If you start recording a route, the app detects the location every few seconds, even with the screen off, until you stop it; a persistent notification reminds you that recording is on.

To get forecasts, routes, maps and prices, the app sends the coordinates of the places involved (not your name or other personal data) directly to the public services listed below, without going through any server of ours.

Third-party services

Each operates under its own privacy policy:

  • MET Norway (api.met.no), NOAA / National Weather Service (api.weather.gov), Environment and Climate Change Canada (api.weather.gc.ca), Bright Sky / DWD (api.brightsky.dev) — weather forecasts, also along routes and in the widgets.
  • Open-Meteo (customer-api.open-meteo.com and related services) — only if you enter your own key for an Open-Meteo commercial plan in the settings: weather, sea, air quality and pollen forecasts (Copernicus CAMS data) and the altitude of places.
  • OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org) — map tiles; Photon / Komoot (photon.komoot.io) — place and address search; FOSSGIS / OSRM (routing.openstreetmap.de) — route calculation; FOSSGIS / Valhalla (valhalla1.openstreetmap.de) — motorbike routes without motorways.
  • Overpass API (overpass-api.de, maps.mail.ru) — towns around the starting point, to suggest a trip.
  • Wikipedia / Wikidata / Wikimedia (wikipedia.org, wikidata.org, upload.wikimedia.org) — descriptions and photos of the things to see in trip suggestions.
  • Google Maps — only when you choose to open a place or route in the Google Maps app.
  • Italian Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy (carburanti.mise.gov.it) — fuel prices of nearby stations (Italy).
  • DuckDuckGo — only if you tap "Webcam", the search opens in the browser.
  • MeteoAlarm / EUMETNET (feeds.meteoalarm.org) — official weather warnings in Europe: the app downloads the country list, without sending coordinates. In the United States and Germany warnings come from NOAA and Bright Sky, with the coordinates of the point.
  • Device geocoding (Google) — to derive country, region and town from a location: vehicle region, weather-warning area, place of diary moments.
  • Google AdMob — shows an optional "rewarded" video ad to unlock Premium for the session. AdMob may collect the advertising identifier and technical device data for ad selection and measurement. In the European Union, EEA, UK and Switzerland, your consent is requested through Google's standard form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is shown. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app offers no in-app purchases and handles no payment data.

Children

The app is not specifically directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights

Since the data stays on your device, you can change or delete it at any time in the app, by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling it. You can revoke permissions (location, Bluetooth, camera, notifications) in Android settings. For advertising identifiers managed by Google, you can manage ad preferences in your device settings or at https://adssettings.google.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app or legal requirements change. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the latest version.
