Privacy policy — AppLock

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy describes how the AppLock app (net.iappscl.applock), developed by Luigi Casella / IAppsCL ("we"), handles data when you use it.

What the app does

AppLock locks the apps you choose: when you open one of them, an unlock screen appears first. For each app you choose how to unlock it: with the master PIN, with a password, with your fingerprint (or the phone's biometric recognition), by solving a sum, or with a time window during which the app does not open at all.

Accessibility service

To work, AppLock uses the Android AccessibilityService API only to know which app is opening (the package name of the window in the foreground), so it can show the unlock screen of the apps you chose.

The app does not read screen content (the service is configured without access to window content), does not watch what you type, does not perform actions on your behalf and does not collect or transmit any of this information. Before you turn it on, the app shows you a notice with these explanations. The service only runs if you enable it in the Android accessibility settings, and you can turn it off at any time.

Data processed and where it stays

The app works only on your device. It needs no account, we run no server and we do not receive or store your data.

  • Locked apps and rules — the list of apps you lock, the unlock method and the time windows are stored in the app's private storage.
  • PIN and password — they are never stored in plain text: the app only keeps a cryptographic hash (PBKDF2 with a random salt), from which the PIN or password cannot be recovered.
  • Fingerprint — recognition happens entirely in the Android system (BiometricPrompt): the app only receives the result "recognised or not" and never sees or stores your fingerprint or any other biometric data.
  • List of installed apps — the app reads the list of apps with a launcher icon only to show it to you when you choose what to lock; it does not store or send it.
  • Settings — language, theme, math difficulty and the expiry of the 24 ad-free hours are stored on the device.

For security, this data is excluded from Android automatic backup: it stays only on the phone until you delete it or uninstall the app.

We do not sell, share or transmit the data listed above to third parties.

Third-party services

The app is free. It includes Google AdMob, which operates under its own privacy policy:

  • a banner at the bottom of the app unlock screen;
  • an optional video ("rewarded") which, once watched, lets you lock more than 3 apps and removes the banner for 24 hours.

AdMob may collect the advertising ID and technical device data to select and measure ads, under Google's rules. In the European Union, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland, before any ad is shown you are asked for consent through Google's standard form (User Messaging Platform); without the form's approval the app loads no ads and still works. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app offers no in-app purchases.

Limits

AppLock cannot stop the phone's owner from uninstalling it or turning off the service in the Android settings: it is not meant as a tool to monitor other people, but to protect your own apps.

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect data about them. In Italy 14 is the minimum age to consent alone to online services (art. 2-quinquies of the Italian Privacy Code); in other EU countries it can be up to 16: below that age, consent to personalised ads must be given by the holder of parental responsibility.

Data controller, legal bases and transfers

The data controller is Luigi Casella (IAppsCL), independent developer, reachable at IAppsCL@gmail.com. No data protection officer (DPO) has been appointed, as it is not required for this type of processing.

  • Data that stays on the device (rules, cryptographic hash of PIN and password, settings): we do not receive or process it; you manage it on your phone.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, personalised ads and the use of the advertising ID are based on your consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR and art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code), collected with Google's form at first launch. For non-personalised ads, measurement and fraud prevention Google may rely on legitimate interest (art. 6.1.f GDPR), which you can object to from the same form. Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects.
  • Emails you send us: we use them only to reply (art. 6.1.b and 6.1.f GDPR) and keep them for as long as needed to handle the request.

Transfers outside the EU: Google may process advertising data in the United States as well. The transfer is based on the European Commission's adequacy decision on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, to which Google LLC adheres, and on standard contractual clauses.

Changing or withdrawing consent: where the law requires it, the app Settings include "Ad privacy choices", which reopens Google's form. You can change your choices or withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal does not make earlier processing unlawful.

Your rights

Since rules and settings stay on your device, you can delete them at any time by removing the locks in the app, clearing the app data in the Android settings or uninstalling it. For advertising identifiers managed by Google, you can manage your ad preferences in your Android device settings or at https://adssettings.google.com.

Under articles 15-22 of the GDPR you have the right to request access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. To exercise them, write to IAppsCL@gmail.com; for data collected by Google you can also contact Google directly. If you believe the processing breaks the law, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it) or with the supervisory authority of the country where you live.
