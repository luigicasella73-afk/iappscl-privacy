Privacy Policy — Ochre

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy describes how the Ochre app (net.iappscl.ochre), developed by Luigi Casella / IAppsCL ("we"), handles data when you use it.

What the app does

Ochre is a colored sand puzzle: you pour layers from one hourglass to another and flip the hourglasses until each color is in a single one. There is a level journey in six stages and a daily challenge that is the same for everyone. Levels, graphics and music are generated on the phone, with no server. The game is free.

Data processed and where it stays

The app works on your device. It does not require an account, we do not run a server that receives your data and we do not store it.

  • Progress — level reached, stars, daily challenges played, streak of days, games in progress and tips already seen are saved in the app's private storage on the device. They stay there until you reset them from Settings, clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Settings — language, music, sound effects, vibration, reminder, color symbols and pouring arrows are saved on the device.
  • Sharing — when you tap "Share", the result (or an invitation to try the game) is passed to the app you choose; we do not receive it.
  • Review — after some games the app may invite you to leave a review using the standard Google Play window. What you write goes to Google Play, not to us.

Progress and settings may be included in the automatic Android backup of your Google account, which remains under your control.

We do not sell, share or transmit the data listed above to third parties.

Third-party services

The app is free and contains ads. It integrates one Google service, which operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — shows optional rewarded video ads, which you watch only if you want: to see the right move (hint), to get five more undos or to add an empty hourglass. It also shows a full-screen ad ("interstitial") during breaks: every two levels completed and when leaving a finished game, never during play, never less than three minutes after the previous one and never in the first two days of use. AdMob may collect the advertising ID and technical device data to select and measure ads, according to Google's policies. In the European Union, EEA, UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent through Google's standard form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is shown. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

No in-app purchases are offered and we do not collect payment data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect data about them. In Italy 14 is the minimum age to consent to online services on one's own (art. 2-quinquies of the Italian Privacy Code); in other EU countries it can be up to 16: below that age, consent to personalised ads must be given by the holder of parental responsibility.

Data controller, legal bases and transfers

The data controller is Luigi Casella (IAppsCL), an independent developer, reachable at IAppsCL@gmail.com. No data protection officer (DPO) has been appointed, as one is not required for this kind of processing.

  • Data that stays on your device (content, settings, history): we do not receive or process it; you manage it on your phone.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, personalised ads and the use of the advertising ID are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and the ePrivacy rules), collected through Google's form on first launch. For non-personalised ads, measurement and fraud prevention Google may rely on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), which you can object to in the same form. Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects.
  • Emails you send us: used only to reply to you (Art. 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f) GDPR) and kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

Transfers outside the EU: Google may process advertising data in the United States. Such transfers rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, to which Google LLC is certified, and on standard contractual clauses.

Changing or withdrawing consent: where the law requires it, the app's Settings include "Ad privacy choices", which reopens Google's form. You can change your choices or withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before.

Your rights

Because progress and settings stay on your device, you can delete them at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling the app. For advertising identifiers handled by Google, you can manage your ad preferences in your Android device settings or at https://adssettings.google.com.

Under Articles 15-22 GDPR you have the right to request access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. To exercise them, write to IAppsCL@gmail.com; for data collected by Google you can also contact Google directly. If you believe the processing breaches the law, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it) or with the supervisory authority of the country where you live.
