Privacy Policy — OneTool

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This privacy policy describes how OneTool (net.iappscl.onetool), developed by Luigi Casella / IAppsCL ("we"), handles data when you use it.

What the app does

OneTool bundles more than 20 practical tools (ruler, level, compass, sound meter, stopwatch, metronome, QR scanner/generator, flashlight, calculator, password generator and more) in a single app, organized by category.

Data processed and where it stays

Every tool works entirely on your device. We don't run a server and we don't receive or store your data.

  • Camera — used by the tools that need it (magnifier, QR scanner, flashlight/flash, heart rate). The image is processed in real time on the device and is not saved or transmitted, except when you generate a QR code and choose to save the image to your phone's gallery.
  • Microphone — used by the sound meter to measure sound level in real time. Audio is not recorded to a file or transmitted anywhere.
  • Location (GPS) — used by the compass, altimeter and speedometer to show you navigation/altitude/speed data in real time. We don't keep a history of your locations and we don't transmit it to third parties.
  • Notifications and alarms — used by the timer to alert you when it expires; handled entirely on the device.

We don't sell, share, or transmit the data listed above to third parties.

Third-party services

The app integrates two Google services, which operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — shows advertising (including an optional "rewarded" ad to unlock premium tools). AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and technical device data for ad selection and measurement. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, you're asked for consent via Google's standard form (User Messaging Platform) before ads are shown. You can review your choices at any time from the app's settings. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — handles the one-time "Premium" purchase that unlocks all tools without ads. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we don't see or store your card or payment method data.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights

Since operating data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app. For advertising identifiers managed by Google, you can manage your ad preferences from your Android device settings or at adssettings.google.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app or legal requirements change. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent version.
