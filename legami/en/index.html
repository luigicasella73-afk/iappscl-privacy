Privacy Policy — Legami

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy describes how the Legami app (net.iappscl.legami), developed by Luigi Casella / IAppsCL ("we"), handles data when you use it.

What the app does

Legami is a word game in Italian: five clues and one hidden word that links them all. Every day there is a new puzzle, the same for everyone, plus a free practice mode. The game is free.

Data handled and where it stays

The app works on your device. It does not require an account, we do not run a server that receives your data, and we do not store it.

  • Puzzles, streak and statistics — the puzzles you played, your day streak and your statistics are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They stay there until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Settings — language, theme, daily reminder and key vibration are saved on your device.
  • Sharing your result — happens only if you tap "Share": the app hands the text to the app you choose and does not send it to any server.
  • Puzzle list — the app may download the updated puzzle list from the IAppsCL website. This is the plain reading of a public file: no personal data is sent.

Puzzles, statistics and settings may be included in Android's automatic backup to your Google account, which remains under your control.

We do not sell, share or transmit the data listed above to third parties.

Third-party services

The app is free and contains ads. It integrates a Google service, which operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — shows optional "rewarded" video ads, which you watch only if you want to: to reveal a letter, to get a second chance or to unlock a past day you did not play. It also shows a full-screen ("interstitial") ad during breaks: at most once a day after the daily puzzle and now and then during practice, never during play and never in your first days of use. AdMob may collect the advertising ID and technical device data to select and measure ads, according to Google's policies. In the European Union, EEA, UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent through Google's standard form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is shown. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

No in-app purchase is offered and we collect no payment data.

Children

The app is not specifically directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights

Because puzzles, statistics and settings stay on your device, you can delete them at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling the app. For advertising identifiers handled by Google, you can manage your ad preferences in your Android device settings or at https://adssettings.google.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app or legal requirements change. The "last updated" date at the top of the document reflects the latest version.
