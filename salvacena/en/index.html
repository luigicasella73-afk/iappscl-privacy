Privacy Policy — Salva Cena

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy describes how the Salva Cena app (net.iappscl.salvacena), developed by Luigi Casella / IAppsCL ("we"), handles data when you use it.

What the app does

Salva Cena suggests what to cook based on the ingredients you have, the number of diners and a calorie limit per person. It includes a recipe catalog, a pantry with expiry dates and spoken step-by-step instructions. Some features are free; the full catalog, the pantry and your personal archives are unlocked with Premium.

Data processed and where it stays

The app works on your device. It does not require an account, we do not run a server, and we do not receive or store your data. The recipe catalog is bundled with the app and works offline.

  • Ingredients, personal recipes and pantry — the ingredients you enter, the recipes you create or edit and the pantry items with their expiry dates are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Settings — language, theme, background and preferences are stored on your device.
  • Voice input — when you dictate ingredients, the app uses the speech recognition service installed on your phone (usually Google's) and only receives the recognized text. The app does not record or keep any audio.
  • Receipt photo — if you scan a receipt to fill the pantry, the photo is taken with your phone's camera app and read on the device with Google ML Kit text recognition, without being sent anywhere. The photo is deleted right after it is read. The app keeps only the items you confirm and, to improve later scans, the corrections you make to product names.
  • Read-aloud recipes — uses your phone's text-to-speech engine, on the device.
  • Notifications — if you allow them, the app notifies you on the device when a pantry item is about to expire.

Ingredients, recipes, pantry and settings may be included in Android's automatic backup to your Google account, which remains under your control.

We do not sell, share or transmit the data listed above to third parties.

Features that open other apps

Some features hand a piece of text to another app, only when you tap the button:

  • Generate with AI — builds a request with the chosen ingredients, diners and calorie limit and passes it to the AI assistant you pick (for example Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot or Perplexity), or to its website if the app is not installed. From then on the text is handled by that service under its own privacy policy. You can then share the generated recipe back to Salva Cena to save it among your recipes.
  • Search online — opens your browser with a DuckDuckGo search for the recipe name.

Third-party services

The app integrates Google services, which operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — shows an optional "rewarded" video ad to unlock Premium features for the current session. AdMob may collect the advertising ID and technical device data to select and measure ads, according to Google's policies. In the European Union, EEA, UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent through Google's standard form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is shown. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — handles, when available, the "Premium" in-app purchase, which permanently unlocks all features. Payment and purchase data are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your card or payment method details.
  • Google ML Kit (text recognition) — reads the receipt on the device. The model is downloaded through Google Play; images are not sent to Google.

Children

The app is not specifically directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights

Since your data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time from the app (recipes, ingredients and pantry items), by clearing the app's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling it. For advertising identifiers managed by Google, you can manage your ad preferences in your Android device settings or at https://adssettings.google.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app or legal requirements change. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the latest version.
