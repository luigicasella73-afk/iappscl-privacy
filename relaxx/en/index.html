Privacy Policy — Relaxx

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy describes how the Relaxx app (net.iappscl.relaxx), developed by Luigi Casella / IAppsCL ("we"), handles data when you use it.

What the app does

Relaxx plays relaxing music and nature sounds, generated (synthesized) in real time on your device. You can blend several sounds with the mixer, create playlists and keep listening with the screen off. The first 2 music tracks and the first 2 sounds are free; all the others are unlocked with Premium.

Data handled and where it stays

The app works entirely on your device. It does not require an account, we do not run a server, and we do not receive or store your data. Audio is generated on the phone: no streaming, no microphone use, no recording.

  • Playlists — playlist names and the tracks they contain are saved in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Mixer and playback — mixer volumes, the sounds switched on and the repeat setting are saved on your device.
  • Settings — language and theme are saved on your device.

This data stays there until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. It may be included in Android's automatic backup to your Google account, which remains under your control.

We do not sell, share or transmit the data listed above to third parties.

Third-party services

The app is free; the music tracks and sounds beyond the first two are a Premium feature. The app integrates a Google service, which operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — shows an optional "rewarded" video ad to unlock all music tracks and all sounds for the current session. AdMob may collect the advertising ID and technical device data to select and measure ads, according to Google's policies. In the European Union, EEA, UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent through Google's standard form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is shown. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app offers no in-app purchases and handles no payment data.

Listening to music and sounds does not use the network.

Children

The app is not specifically directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights

Because playlists and settings stay on your device, you can delete them at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling the app. For advertising identifiers handled by Google, you can manage your ad preferences in your Android device settings or at https://adssettings.google.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app or legal requirements change. The "last updated" date at the top of the document reflects the latest version.
