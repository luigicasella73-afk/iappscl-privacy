Privacy Policy — Avvisami

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Avvisami (net.iappscl.avvisami), developed by Luigi Casella / IAppsCL ("we"), handles data when you use it.

What the app does

Avvisami reminds you of the deadlines that matter: tax deadlines, vehicles (car tax, insurance, inspection, service), appointments, health, identity documents, memos and custom reminders, with configurable advance notifications. It includes a "Garage" for your vehicles and a "Properties" registry, with indicative estimates of the Italian car tax (bollo) and property tax (IMU).

Data processed and where it stays

All processing happens entirely on your device. The app requires no account, we don't run a server and we don't receive or store your data.

  • Deadlines, vehicles and properties — what you enter (titles, dates, amounts, notes, places, document number and issuing authority, plate, vehicle technical data, odometer, property address and cadastral data) is stored in the app's local database on the device. Car tax and IMU estimates are calculated on the device, without sending data anywhere. The data stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Approximate location — if you grant the permission, the app reads the device's last known approximate location (never in the background, never continuously) only to prefill the vehicle's region or the property's municipality. To turn coordinates into a region/municipality it uses Android's system geocoding service, which may contact Google services. The location is not stored or transmitted to us. You can deny the permission and fill in the fields manually.
  • Notifications and exact alarms — used only to show your reminders at the scheduled time; after the phone restarts, the app reschedules the reminders already set.
  • Places and Google Maps — if you open a place in Google Maps, the app launches Maps (or the browser) passing it the place text; from then on Google's privacy policy applies. If you share a place from Maps to Avvisami, the app receives the shared text and copies it into the "Place" field of the reminder you are editing.
  • Settings — language, theme, background and preferences are stored on the device and may be included in Android's automatic backup to your Google account. Deadlines and Premium status are not included in this backup.

We don't sell, share, or transmit the data listed above to third parties.

Third-party services

The app integrates Google services, which operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — shows an optional "rewarded" video ad to unlock Premium features for the current session. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and technical device data for ad selection and measurement. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, you're asked for consent via Google's standard form (User Messaging Platform) before ads are shown. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Premium — there are no in-app purchases: Premium features are unlocked only by watching a short video, for the current session. We don't process any payment data.
  • Google Maps — opened only at your request, as described above.

Tax estimates

Car tax and IMU estimates are indicative and are not an official tax calculation: rates, tariffs and reliefs may vary by region, municipality or personal situation. Always check the amounts with official sources or your advisor.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect data about them. In Italy 14 is the minimum age to consent to online services on one's own (Art. 2-quinquies of the Italian Privacy Code); in other EU countries it can be up to 16: below that age, consent to personalised ads must be given by a parent or guardian.

Data controller, legal bases and transfers

The data controller is Luigi Casella (IAppsCL), an independent developer, reachable at IAppsCL@gmail.com. No data protection officer (DPO) has been appointed, as one is not required for this kind of processing.

  • Data that stays on your device (content, settings, history): we do not receive or process it; you manage it on your phone.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, personalised ads and the use of the advertising ID are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and the ePrivacy rules), collected through Google's form on first launch. For non-personalised ads, measurement and fraud prevention Google may rely on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), which you can object to in the same form. Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects.
  • Emails you send us: used only to reply to you (Art. 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f) GDPR) and kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

Transfers outside the EU: Google may process advertising data in the United States. Such transfers rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, to which Google LLC is certified, and on standard contractual clauses.

Changing or withdrawing consent: where the law requires it, the app's Settings include "Ad privacy choices", which reopens Google's form. You can change your choices or withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before.

Your rights

Since operational data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time from the app, by clearing the app's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling it. For advertising identifiers managed by Google, you can manage your ad preferences from your Android device settings or at adssettings.google.com.

Under Articles 15-22 GDPR you have the right to request access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. To exercise them, write to IAppsCL@gmail.com; for data collected by Google you can also contact Google directly. If you believe the processing breaches the law, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it) or with the supervisory authority of the country where you live.
