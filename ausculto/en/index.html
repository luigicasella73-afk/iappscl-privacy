Privacy Policy — Ausculto

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy describes how the Ausculto app (net.iappscl.ausculto), developed by Luigi Casella / IAppsCL ("we"), handles data when you use it.

What the app does

Ausculto is a stethoscope for the machines in your home: washing machine, fridge, car, bike, fans. Using the phone's microphone and accelerometer, it learns the sound and vibration of a machine while it is healthy (the "fingerprint") and, in later checks, tells you whether something has changed. It also includes a spectrogram, a vibration meter, a tachometer and a spirit level. All analysis happens on the phone, with no server.

Data handled and where it stays

The app works on your device. It does not require an account, we do not run a server that receives your data and we do not store it.

  • Microphone audio — during a check (10, 20 or 30 seconds, started by you) the app analyses the sound to compute its spectrum and other measurements. The microphone is never on in the background and the audio is not sent to anyone.
  • Recordings — for the "before / now" comparison the app keeps a short low-quality recording (16 kHz) of the fingerprints and of the last 10 checks of each machine, in the app's private storage. Older recordings are deleted automatically. If someone speaks near the phone during a check, their voice may end up in the recording: it still stays only on your device.
  • Vibrations — the accelerometer measures the vibrations of the machine the phone rests on. The values stay on the device.
  • Machines and checks — machine names and types, fingerprints, check results and health score are saved in the app's private storage.
  • Settings — check duration, vibration and the spirit level calibration are saved on the device.

You can delete a check, a machine or all data from within the app (Settings → "Delete all data"). This data may be included in Android's automatic backup to your Google account, which remains under your control.

We do not sell, share or transmit the data listed above to third parties.

Third-party services

The app is free and contains ads. It integrates a Google service, which operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — shows an optional "rewarded" video ad, which you watch only if you want to: it unlocks Premium (unlimited machines, vibration meter, tachometer and before/now listening) until the app is closed. There are no banners and no full-screen ads. AdMob may collect the advertising ID and technical device data to select and measure ads, according to Google's policies. It does not receive the audio or the measurements of your machines. In the European Union, EEA, UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent through Google's standard form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is shown. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

No in-app purchases are offered and we do not collect payment data.

Limits of the measurements

A phone is not a certified measuring instrument: levels, ISO zones and the health score are indicative. Ausculto tells you when something changes, but it does not replace a check by a technician.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect data about them. In Italy 14 is the minimum age to consent to online services on one's own (art. 2-quinquies of the Italian Privacy Code); in other EU countries it can be up to 16: below that age, consent to personalised ads must be given by the holder of parental responsibility.
